I
was trying to sign up for Hoopla with my library card.  My pin is
only 4 digits long, but it required an 8 digit password.   Do I
have to change it on the library site?
When
registering for Hoopla, it will ask for your library card number and PIN (4
digits) and then prompt you to enter your email address and password (8-40
characters).  If you do need to reset your library card PIN, you can call
513-369-6900 or visit your local library branch.
