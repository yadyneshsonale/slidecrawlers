Course objectives:
A second course that is
designed to give students a basic understanding of Discrete
Mathematics and its role in Computer Science. Computers handle
discrete data rather than continuous data. The course presents an
overview of some of the major theoretical concepts needed to analyze
this type of data.
Topics covered include:
Counting,
sequences and sums, discrete probability, basic statistics,
recurrence relations, randomized algorithms. Material is illustrated
through examples from computing.
Textbook:
Discrete Structures for Computer Science: Counting, Recursion,
and Probability by Michiel Smid
Additional material: Discrete Mathematics and its
Applications, by Rosen. This book contains a large number of
exercises as well as solutions. Do not buy this book, because it
is too expensive. We used to use this book for COMP 1805, so you
may still have an old copy, or you may borrow a copy from a
friend.
