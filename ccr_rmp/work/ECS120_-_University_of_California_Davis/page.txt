Daveâs Prereq Advice and Historical Grade Trends
Technically the single required prerequisite (ECS 20: Discrete Mathematics) introduces all the mathematical concepts you need for this course.
However, our Data Structures classes (ECS 32B, 36C) provide intuition-building practice (
e.g.
, manipulating discrete objects like graphs, trees and lists in code).
Historically, UCD CS/ECE students who previously took a course on data structures have performed roughly half a letter grade better in ECS 120.
Math majors tend to grasp the formal definitions and proofs well without studying data structures.
