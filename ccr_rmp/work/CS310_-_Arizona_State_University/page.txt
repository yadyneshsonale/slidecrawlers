Outcomes
Apply appropriate algorithmic approaches to problems.
Analyze running time using asymptotic notation.
Appreciate CS theory; prepare for technical interviews.
Give/receive constructive critiques; collaborate with modern
tools.
Study Tips
Mile wide, foot deep — do not fall behind.
Ask questions; be active.
Practice consistently to master problem solving.
Recommended Books
Introduction to Algorithms (CLRS)
Cormen, Leiserson, Rivest, Stein — 4th Edition, MIT Press.
Rigorous and comprehensive.
Algorithm Design
Jon Kleinberg, Éva Tardos — 1st Edition, Pearson. Design paradigms
and intuition.
Grokking Algorithms
Aditya Bhargava — 1st Edition, Manning. Visual and
beginner-friendly.
