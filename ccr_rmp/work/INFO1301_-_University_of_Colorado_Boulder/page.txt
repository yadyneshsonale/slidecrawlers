OpenIntro Statistics

Surveys concepts and techniques for characterizing and quantifying data.
Students will learn to use different types of quantitative data,
to summarize data with descriptive statistics, to measure similarity of
different datasets, to interpret probabilities and statistical significance
and to quantify and predict changes in data.
No prerequisites.
