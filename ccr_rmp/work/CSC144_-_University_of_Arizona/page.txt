The text for this semester is
Discrete Mathematics and Its Applications, 8th ed.
,
by Kenneth H. Rosen, 2019.
Do not buy an international edition or an earlier edition!
Instead...
This book is available as an ebook via the
Pay One Price
program, or you can also get it from the publisher as an ebook
or in print.
You might also wish to consider purchasing the companion
Student Solutions Guide
,
which offers detailed solutions to the
text's odd-numbered end-of-section exercises.
Kneel Before Z
odd

cover my lecture material through roughly the first half of the old
CSc 245 course.  Much of it is also applicable to this class.

Piazza

There are some other online textbooks that cover parts of the
material in this class:
Connecting Discrete Mathematics and Computer Science
by
David Liben-Nowell
.
This is a pre-print version a now-published textbook.
"Book of Proof"
by
Richard Hammack
.
The name's a bit misleading; it covers a good amount of the
material for a discrete structures course, including proofs.
An Active Introduction to Discrete Mathematics and Algorithms (V3.0)
by
Charles A. Cusack
.
A discrete math text based on parts of another author's writings.
Introduction to Higher Mathematics
by Patrick Keef
and
David Guichard
.
Logic, proofs, functions, and relations, plus some number theory,
'cause ... why not?
How To Write Proofs
by
Larry W. Cusick
.
And that's what it covers.
