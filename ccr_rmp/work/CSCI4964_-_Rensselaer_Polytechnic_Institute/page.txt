Sunday October 31st at 4:00pm
Images for the puzzle piece recognition program are available in
~stewart/images/puzzle
The files named
piece*.pgm
are the models and the others are
the images in which your program should recognize the pieces.   In
some of them, your program will not succeed.  You should try to
understand why, though!
One problem with the edge detectors is that they do not break
edges up at corners.  I will be providing such a technique by
midweek. (Actually, Friday, see above!)  As an alternative, you might
want to consider running a line detector after running the edge
detector.  Use a low threshold on the line length, though.
