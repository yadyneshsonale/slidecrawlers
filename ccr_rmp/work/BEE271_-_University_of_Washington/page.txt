BEE 271 Digital circuits and systems Spring 2017
This is archive of the materials I used the last time I taught this course, spring quarter 2017.
Contact me if you'd like the original PowerPoint or Word documents.
My objective when teaching this class was that students should leave sufficiently skilled they could pass a
typical whiteboard interview for an entry-level position in digital design requiring basic competency at
both gate level design and using Verilog.
