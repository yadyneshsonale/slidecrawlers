Specific objectives of this course are:
To introduce the student to the data structures and algorithms that
will be met in advanced courses in computer science.
To introduce the student to abstraction as a tool for
representing problems and problem solving.
To introduce the student to algorithmic analysis as an
essential part of problem solving.
To introduce the student to the formal analysis of algorithms.
To describe the major problem-solving strategies used in the design of
algorithms.
