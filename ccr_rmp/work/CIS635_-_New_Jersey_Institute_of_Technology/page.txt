NOTE:  All homework's must be typed.   At the end of the term
each group will submit a CD with each of the group assignments on it and which
has a subdirectory for each group member.  The contents of each
subdirectory will be a copy of the group members homework assignments.
Therefore, you need to do all assignments as a word or computer readable
file.  There will be one CD per group.
IF
THE CD IS NOT HANDED IN WITH THE GROUP PROJECT ON IT, IT WILL RESULT IN AN
F FOR THE PROJECT.
IF
A GROUP MEMBER DOES NOT HAVE HIS ASSIGNMENTS IN A SUB-DIRECTORY ON THAT CD THEN
THAT GROUP MEMBER WILL RECEIVE AN I UNTIL A COMPUTER READABLE COPY IS
HANDED IN
AND
THAT GROUP MEMBERS GRADE WILL BE LOWERED BY 1/2 GRADE.
There
are two

A
breakers

in this
course
:
1. Missing a
homework assignment or it is late without prior permission.
2. Missing a
class without notifying me before the class.
If
either of these happen
YOU WILL NOT
receive an A no matter
how well you do otherwise.
Also
note if you do hand in late homework, the maximum grade you can receive for it
is a 70.
