Course Description
This is what the catalog says:
This course provides the student with an understanding of the basic
components
of a general-purpose operating system.
Topics include processes, process management, synchronization, input/
output devices and their programming, interrupts, memory management,
resource allocation, and an introduction to file systems.
Students will be expected to design and implement a large piece of
system
software.
Intended audience: computer science majors and others interested in
studying
the software and hardware components of computer systems.
Undergraduate credit may not be earned both for this course and for
CS
502
.
Recommended background:
CS
2303
or
CS
2301
, and
CS
2011
.
Textbook
Operating System Concepts, Seventh Edition, Abraham Silberschatz,
Peter Baer Galvin, Greg Gagne. John Wiley & Sons, Inc.
