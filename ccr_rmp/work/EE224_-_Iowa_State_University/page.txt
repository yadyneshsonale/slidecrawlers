Basic Course Information
E E 224. Signals and Systems I.
(3-3) Cr. 4. F.S.
Prereq: 201, Math 267. Mathematical Preliminaries. Introduction to signals and
systems. Signal manipulations. System properties. LTI systems. Impulse response
and convolution. Fourier Series representation and properties of Fourier Series.
Continuous and discrete-time Fourier Transforms and their properties.
Applications and demonstrations using Matlab.
