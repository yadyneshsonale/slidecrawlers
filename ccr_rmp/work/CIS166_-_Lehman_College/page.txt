CIS 166: Computer Programming for Information Processing I
Lehman College, City University of New York
Spring 2015
CIS 166 and CMP 230 both cover introductory programming in Python.  The programming and assessments differ, but instructors are knowledgable in the content for both classes, and you are welcome and encouraged to
attend any of office hours, tutoring hours, and review sessions (anouncements will be sent via Blackboard to students registered in the class).
Note that the university has approved the renumbering of CMP 230 to CMP 167 in Spring 2014.
The course appears as CMP 167 in some official documents and will appear on transcripts and
CUNYFirst with the new number as of Spring 2015.
