Course Description.
We will explore techniques for designing and analyzing efficient algorithms, including recurrence relations and divide-and-conquer algorithms, dynamic programming, graph algorithms (e.g., network flow), amortized analysis, cache-efficient and external-memory algorithms, high probability bounds and randomized algorithms, parallel algorithms and multithreaded computations, NP-completeness and approximation algorithms, the alpha technique, and FFT ( Fast Fourier Transforms ).
Prerequisites.
Some background in algorithms analysis (e.g., CSE 373) and programming languages (e.g., C/C++) is required (or consent of instructor).
