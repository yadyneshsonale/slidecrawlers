Best Time To Visit Arizona

  • Spring: One of the best seasons for desert wildflowers, hiking, scenic drives, lakes, and outdoor sightseeing.
  • Summer: Ideal for high-country escapes in Flagstaff, the White Mountains, Rim Country, and Arizona lake recreation.
  • Fall: Excellent for road trips, historic towns, fishing, camping, cooler desert weather, and colorful mountain scenery.
  • Winter: Popular for Phoenix, Tucson, desert parks, golf, museums, wildlife viewing, and mild-weather vacations.

FAQ

What is Arizona Leisure?
Arizona Leisure is a travel and vacation planning guide with information about Arizona attractions, lakes, maps, scenic drives, outdoor recreation, historic sites, and things to do.

What are the most popular places to visit in Arizona?
Popular Arizona destinations include the Grand Canyon, Sedona, Phoenix, Flagstaff, Lake Powell, Monument Valley, Tombstone, Prescott, and the White Mountains.

What is the best time to visit Arizona?
Spring and fall are ideal for statewide travel, summer is best for mountain and lake destinations, and winter is popular for desert cities like Phoenix and Tucson.

Does Arizona Leisure include maps and directions?
Yes, the site includes Arizona maps, area maps, distance guides, and directions to many attractions, towns, lakes, parks, and scenic destinations.

Does Arizona Leisure cover outdoor recreation?
